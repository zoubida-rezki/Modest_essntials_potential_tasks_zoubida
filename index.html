Marketing, Advertising, and Social Media

Questions and ideas for understanding customers, increasing brand awareness, and improving ad performance.

Understanding Current Customer Trends and Interests

How can we simplify the process of answering the question, “What is trending right now?” Google Trends is broad and doesn't tell us exactly what people interested in modest or halal fashion are looking for. How can we consistently understand what our target customers want and what they want to see?

Current direction: Find ways to build stronger relationships with loyal customers to better understand their current and future preferences and tailor our offerings to them. This could also help us understand what people are searching for and use those insights to optimize the website for SEO and GEO.

Concrete ideas: Not yet defined.

Instagram Engagement and Brand Awareness

Despite having a large number of followers, the account receives relatively little engagement on its posts. How can we increase engagement, stay top of mind with potential customers, and encourage people to talk about and recommend the brand to others?

Perhaps we could create content that people are likely to repost, remember, and discuss with their friends and family.

Idea: Create a post responding to this influencer's complaints about modest clothing brands by showcasing Modest Essentials pieces that challenge the typical expectations of modest fashion brands.

Advertising Performance and Strategy

There are currently six ads, all from August 22–24, ranging from simple product displays to short-form content with captions that relate to customers. Which types of ads are most likely to grab customers' attention and generate clicks to the website? How can we create ads that attract potential customers, resonate with them, reflect our brand, and ultimately make them more likely to buy?

Current direction: Use performance data to test new strategies, measure results, and focus resources on the approaches that generate the most customers and sales. Understand which ads perform best, when they perform best, and why, then use those insights to recreate successful engagement.

Website Optimization

Potential improvements to the website's design, product discovery, and customer experience.

Reviews Page

A separate reviews page exists, even though individual product pages already have reviews. Do customers actually use the dedicated reviews page? If so, how do they interact with it, and does it serve its intended purpose? Is it helpful, or does it add unnecessary friction to the customer experience?

Newsletter Sign-Up and Personalized Recommendations

A newsletter sign-up option exists at the bottom of the website, but visitors aren't prompted to sign up when they first access the site. Unless they scroll all the way down, they may never see it.

If customers create accounts, this information could potentially be used later to personalize product recommendations. For example, customers who frequently purchase bodycon abayas could be shown more bodycon abayas and similar products they are likely to buy.

Header Size

The header takes up approximately one-quarter of the landing page. Is this using too much of the available screen space?

Product Labeling and Categorization

Evaluate how clothing items are named, tagged, and categorized. These choices affect how easily customers can find products through the website's search function and external search engines.

Review Notification Pop-Ups

Do pop-up notifications displaying reviews improve the customer experience, or do they hurt it?

Products Without Reviews

Some items don't have any reviews. Does this negatively affect how potential customers perceive these products when considering a purchase? Should we incentivize customers to leave reviews?

Product Attribution

Why is “By Modest Essentials” displayed next to the product? Does this serve a useful purpose for customers?
